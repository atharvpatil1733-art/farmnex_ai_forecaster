Generated by `python -m forecaster.data` (agmarknet-data-prep). Prices are Rs/quintal, arrivals in tonnes.

## Summary

- Source: **CEDA Agmarknet API cache up to 2025-10-30, then website exports in data/raw/ for later dates only (14656 rows)**
- Raw price rows read: **84666**; raw quantity rows read: **76738**
- Rows in mandi.csv (one per date x market x crop): **79421** (76962 with a price, 2459 arrivals-only)
- Date range: **2012-01-01 to 2026-09-28**
- Markets with real data: 16 (Alephata, Baramati, Indapur, Junnar, Kalyan, Khadki, Khed(Chakan), Manchar, Manjri, Moshi, Narayangaon, Otur, Pimpri, Pune, Shirur, Vashi)
- Crops with real data: Onion, Potato, Tomato
- Rows with arrivals: 68666 (86.5%)

- **Onion**: 29188 priced market-days, months present: 2012-01..2023-11, 2024-01..2026-09; arrivals on 25766 rows
- **Tomato**: 26500 priced market-days, months present: 2012-01..2026-09; arrivals on 24645 rows
